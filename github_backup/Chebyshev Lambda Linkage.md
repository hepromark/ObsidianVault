Features: Explain the reasons behind the specific configuration and why it works for the intended function

Mobility: Provide the number of links, number of joints, types of joints, and then apply Kutzbach’s equation. Also, comment on the Degrees of Freedom (DOF) and provide an interpretation

Motion Recognition: List possible motion categories of bodies, such as rectilinear or curvilinear translation, pure rotation about a fixed point, and complex motion. Only mentioning the link number, name, and type of motion is sufficient for motion recognition

Motion Transmission: Pay attention to how the type of joints or contact points affects motion transmission. For example, explain that the linear velocity of the driving gear at the contact point is exactly transmitted to the other gear if they mesh at that point, and the tangential accelerations at the contact point would be the same (not the normal component). Additionally, comment on other important points in your mechanism

Applications: When discussing a specific mechanism, focus on its applications within that context. For instance, if a knife mechanism is selected, do not write about different applications of a knife in general. Instead, emphasize the type of mechanism used in that specific knife and explore other applications of such a mechanism in different devices
